      How to write it

      Tap the keys or type: / makes a fraction, ^ a power, sqrt, sin, pi and int turn into symbols. Enter starts the next line. Solving an equation? Put it on line 1 and end with x = 2 or x = 3. Ctrl+Enter checks.

        About mathlint

        Type an equation and see it solved step by step — or write out your own working and find your first mistake.

        • Everything runs on your device — nothing you type is sent anywhere.
        • Every answer is checked before it is shown. When mathlint is not sure, it says so.
